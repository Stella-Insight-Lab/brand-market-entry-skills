# Market and regulatory evidence schema

```text
evidence_id
claim
fact / inference / hypothesis
source_url
source_name
source_tier (A/B/C/D)
source_type
country
category
metric_definition
unit
currency
published_date
retrieved_date
time_range
sample_size
calculation
status
confidence
bias_or_limitation
next_validation
```

法规事项额外保留 `jurisdiction`、`product_classification`、`obligation`、`responsible_party`、`required_document`、`authority`、`regulation_version`、`published_date`、`effective_date`、`transition_or_expiry`、`section_or_clause`、`retrieved_date`、`estimated_time`、`estimated_cost`、`professional_review_needed` 和 `veto_flag`。
